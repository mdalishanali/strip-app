Privacy Policy

Strip for Mac · Last updated 1 October 2026

Short version: Strip works entirely on your Mac. It arranges your windows and nothing about them leaves your computer. We don't run analytics, ads or tracking.

Windows on your Mac

With your permission (macOS Accessibility), Strip reads the position, size and type of open windows so it can arrange them. It does not read window contents, what you type, or screen images. None of this information is stored or sent anywhere.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. To check whether you own Strip Pro, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and your App Store purchase history. It does not receive your name, email address or any window information.

What we don't do

We don't collect personal information, use advertising identifiers, track you across apps or websites, or sell data.

Children

Strip is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above. Questions? Contact us.
