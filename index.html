Common questions

Why does Strip need Accessibility access?
macOS only lets apps move and resize other apps' windows through the Accessibility permission. Strip uses it for exactly that. It never reads what's inside your windows or what you type.

How do I move around the strip?
Press ⌥H and ⌥L to focus the window on the left or right. ⌥⇧H and ⌥⇧L move the window itself. ⌥R cycles the width between ⅓, ½ and ⅔, ⌥F makes it full width and ⌥C centers it. Hold ⌥ and scroll to slide the strip. The full list is in Settings → Shortcuts.

How do I stop an app from tiling?
Open Settings → Floating Apps and add it. Or press ⌃⌥T to float or re-tile just the window you're in.

What's free and what's Pro?
The free plan tiles up to four windows. Strip Pro tiles every window on every display, available yearly (with a free trial) or as a one-time lifetime purchase.

How do I restore my purchase on another Mac?
Open the Strip menu in the menu bar, choose Upgrade to Strip Pro…, then Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do I cancel my subscription?
Open the App Store app, click your name, then Account Settings → Subscriptions → Manage.
